OUR STORY

Handmade pieces.
Made to stand out.

Ayoré Craft is a contemporary handmade fashion and accessories brand specializing in handcrafted footwear, bags, leather goods, and fashion accessories.

Our collections include handcrafted shoes and slippers, boots, tote bags, duffel bags, laptop and office bags, purses, belts, and other carefully designed accessories.

We also offer custom-made designs, creating unique pieces tailored to individual preferences and style.

At Ayoré Craft, we combine quality craftsmanship, contemporary design, functionality, and individuality to create pieces that make a statement while remaining practical and timeless.
